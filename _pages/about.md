Welcome to my personal website
-------


I am a former astrophysicist turned ML/AI Instructor. I've developed an extensive background in data science and machine learning during graduate school. This website hosts a plain text version of my resume, as well as summary pages for various machine-learning and artificial intelligence projects I've completed.

I am excited about advances in AI but I am also keen on contributing to the ethical application of AI in all its forms. I am committed to developing AI ethically. I believe it's crucial to develop AI with the sensitivity and attention to avoiding the harmful impacts on marginalized communities. I am eager to collaborate with others and learn from diverse perspectics to ensure the technologies we create are inclusive and respectful to all communities. 

When I am not doing work I will usually do a manner of different fiber arts such as embroidery, crocheting, and beading. I also love reading non-fiction and science fiction such as Ursula K. Leguin and Octavia Butler. Below you'll find pictures of my two, very fuzzy, cats, Harry (13yo) and Vera (7yo).
